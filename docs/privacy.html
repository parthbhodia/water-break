Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: Water Break does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it makes no network requests.

What the extension stores

  • Settings (reminder interval, how long the reminder stays, daily goal, cup size, reminder days, quiet hours, language and theme) are stored with chrome.storage.sync. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync these settings between your devices under Google's own privacy policy. The developer never receives them.
  • Your water log (today's total in ml, the time of your last drink, the last 14 days of daily totals, and a count of reminders shown) and whether the intro tour has been shown are stored with chrome.storage.local on your device only.

Access to web pages

To show the reminder on the page you are viewing, the extension runs a small content script on http and https pages. The script only creates and removes the reminder card. It does not read, collect, store or transmit page content, URLs, form data or browsing history.

Notifications

On pages where the reminder card cannot be shown (for example chrome:// pages, the new tab page or the PDF viewer), Water Break shows a system notification instead.

Third parties

None. The extension does not load remote code, fonts, images, analytics or advertising, and it does not send data to any third party.

Your control

  • Uninstalling the extension removes the data stored on your device.
  • You can clear its data at any time from chrome://extensions.
  • Synced settings can be cleared from your Chrome sync settings.

Children

Water Break does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
